Friendly dog grooming in Listowel.

Local grooming care

Muddy Paws Dog Grooming is a Listowel-based service offering professional dog grooming by appointment. Public business information lists bathing, drying, clipping, styling, nail clipping and ear cleaning among its services.

The business is also publicly listed as a member of the Irish Professional Dog Groomers Association.
