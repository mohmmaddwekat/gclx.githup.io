About

Mobile power washing focused on practical exterior care.

Hot Shot Power Washing serves Port Alberni as a mobile power washing service for common exterior surfaces including houses, decks, driveways and sidewalks.

Mobile by design

The service is set up to come to the customer, making it straightforward to discuss exterior cleaning at the property itself.

Surface protection available

After cleaning, Whitehall sealant can be applied for additional protection and longer-lasting care.

Direct communication

Customers can reach the business directly by phone or email to discuss the work and request a quote.
