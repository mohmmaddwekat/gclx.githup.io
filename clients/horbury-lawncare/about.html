About the business

Local care for real outdoor spaces.

A straightforward garden service focused on practical upkeep, tidy finishes and clear communication.

Horbury Lawncare serves customers across Wakefield, Horbury, Ossett and nearby areas with a broad mix of garden maintenance and exterior-care services.

The work ranges from recurring grass cutting and hedge trimming to garden tidy-ups, turfing, pressure washing, weed treatments, aggregate driveways and soft landscaping.

With domestic and commercial work in the service mix, customers can start with a simple conversation about what the space needs and request a quotation.
