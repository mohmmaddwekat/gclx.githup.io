About D15

Professional grooming with an animal-care background.

D15 Dog Grooming serves dogs in Dublin 15 from Unit C6 at the Base Centre in Mulhuddart. Public business information describes the owner as both a qualified dog groomer and a trained veterinary nurse.

Qualified Groomer

Professional grooming for dogs of all breeds, with full grooming services available.

Veterinary-Nurse Training

Animal-care training complements the practical grooming service and pet-focused approach.

All Breeds

The business publicly states that dogs of all breeds are welcome.

Convenient Contact

Clients can call or email directly, and public information lists free collection and delivery.
