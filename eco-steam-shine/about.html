Local car detailing in Limerick.

Eco Steam & Shine Car Detailing is a Limerick car wash and detailing business founded in 2016. Public listings associate Jakub Socha with the business and list its location at the car park of Limerick Strand Hotel on Ennis Road.

The business can be contacted directly by phone or email for questions and availability.
