About Dirty Beasts

Dirty Beasts Dog Grooming is a Lucan grooming business run by qualified dog groomer Zoe. Public sources describe one-to-one grooming for small, medium and large dogs, alongside canine first responder training and a strong interest in animal care.

Care-first grooming

The aim is straightforward: give each dog focused attention in a calm environment, while providing practical grooming services that help owners keep coats, nails and ears well maintained.

Zoe's animal-care background and continued studies in Animal Science support a thoughtful, pet-focused approach.
