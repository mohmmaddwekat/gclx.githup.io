Frisky Business Pet Grooming is a home-based Edmonton grooming salon for dogs and cats.

Meet Karen Stuart

Karen Stuart owns and operates Frisky Business Pet Grooming. She is a Certified Feline Master Groomer and provides grooming for both dogs and cats in a home-based, appointment-only setting.

The business is located at 4823 11A Avenue NW in Edmonton. Appointments are available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
