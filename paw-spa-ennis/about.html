About PAw Spa

PAw Spa Dog Grooming Salon is an Ennis pet-grooming business associated publicly with owner Silvy. Public listings describe Silvy as a passionate dog groomer with several years of experience and a focus on providing quality grooming care.

The salon is based at Harvey's Quay in Ennis and currently takes enquiries directly by phone and email.
